CORE THEORETICAL VARIABLES OWID CO2 data:

population: Useful for weighting or checking per capita calculations .

gdp: Gross domestic product, your main control variable .

consumption_co2_per_capita: This is key for your analysis because it relates directly to lifestyles and consumption.

co2_per_capita: Production-based emissions for comparison and calculating the emissions gap.

renewables_share_energy: The share of primary energy from renewable sources.

energy_per_capita: Primary energy consumption per capita.

IDENTIFIER COLUMNS:

iso_code: The 3-letter country code. - MAIN MERGE KEY

country: The full country name.

year: The time key for panel data. - TEMPORAL MERGE KEY
